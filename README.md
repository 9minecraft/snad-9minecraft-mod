# Snad — Build Faster Sugar Cane and Nether Wart Farms

Snad 9minecraft Mod adds three new sand blocks that make your crops grow much faster. This mod helps you build quick automatic farms for sugar cane, cactus, bamboo, and nether wart without changing how the base game works.

Three New Blocks: Brings regular Snad, Red Snad, and Suol Snad into the game. Craft these blocks by putting two sand, two red sand, or two soul sand together in your crafting grid. Plants placed on top of these blocks grow faster than they do on normal dirt or sand.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **TheRoBrit**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
